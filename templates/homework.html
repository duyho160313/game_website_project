Grade 8 English Revision

Learn it, flip the cards, then take the quiz.

Parts of speech

Noun (thing), verb (action), adjective (describes a noun), adverb (describes a verb), pronoun (replaces a noun), preposition (position), conjunction (joins).

Tenses

Past: I walked. Present: I walk. Future: I will walk. Present perfect: I have walked. Past perfect: I had walked.

Subject–verb agreement

Singular subject, singular verb: The dog barks. Plural subject, plural verb: The dogs bark.

Its or it’s?

“It’s” means “it is”. “Its” shows ownership: The cat licked its paw.

Literary devices

Simile: compares with “like” or “as”. Metaphor: says one thing is another. Personification: human qualities for objects. Alliteration: repeated first sounds. Hyperbole: exaggeration.

Essay paragraph (PEEL)

Point, Evidence, Explain, Link back to the question.
