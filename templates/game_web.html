Hide & Seek game

Description

Shrink Hide & Seek is a combination of hide and seek and the ability to shrink!

🤏 AS HIDER: Shrink and hide inside objects. Use powers and trolls to distract the seekers. DON’T GET CAUGHT!

👀 AS SEEKER: Try to find the hiders and kill them. Once you kill them they’ll join your team. FIND ALL HIDERS!

💰 Collect coins to get better powers and weapons!
